# The Glamping Experts

Website for The Glamping Experts: a shop for British-made luxury glamping units (shepherd huts, pods, tents, hot tubs, saunas, stoves, communal areas), with safety guides, advice and news. Domains to use: theglampingexperts.co.uk and glampingexperts.co.uk.

## Current state

- `index.html` is the whole site in one file. Pages switch with `#hash` links, which Google indexes poorly, so it needs splitting into real pages (one per category, guide and news story) before launch.
- It carries `<meta name="robots" content="noindex">` and a "Private preview" banner. Remove both only at launch.
- Enquiry and PAT forms do not send anywhere yet.

## Business model (never shown on the site)

Commission only. Makers sell and deliver direct, and pay commission on sales we send them. Never explain supplier arrangements, commissions or our process to customers on the site.

## Copy rules

- Write for the buyer browsing for something to put in their field. Short, benefit-led, no filler, no thinking out loud.
- No product counts, no bundles, no "all in one place", no "delivered to your field".
- Only list a maker's name once that maker has agreed. Use only photos makers have given permission to use.
- News must cite real sources. Never invent facts.
- Follow Chris's non-fiction style guide: no em dashes, complete sentences.

## Style

Chatsworth-inspired: light uppercase sans headings with wide letter-spacing (Jost, as a free stand-in for Azo Sans), stone bands (#e6e4db), deep blue #0A3C4B.
